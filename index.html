About Me

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Hyderabad. I previously graduated from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhilai with the class of 2025.

My academic and research pursuits are deeply rooted in advanced cryptography. I specialize in lattice-based and post-quantum cryptography, exploring the concrete security of modern algorithms. My work also extends into zero-knowledge proof systems, side-channel cryptanalysis, and the architectural design of secure, privacy-preserving protocols.

Research & Projects

  • Post-Quantum Cryptography & ML-KEM/ML-DSA Analysis

    Conducting comprehensive research on the concrete security and parameter bounds of CRYSTALS-Kyber and CRYSTALS-Dilithium. Developed a standalone Python GUI tool utilizing CustomTkinter to estimate classical and quantum security levels, failure probabilities, and BKZ block sizes for post-quantum algorithms.

  • Cyclone: Shielded UTXO Privacy Pool

    Designed and implemented an auditable, traceable version of a shielded privacy pool (inspired by Tornado Cash/Tornado Nova). The project utilizes Circom circuits, Solidity smart contracts, and an in-browser proving client using SnarkJS and Ethers.js.

  • Post-Quantum Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)

    Developed a CBDC protocol architecture featuring anonymous credentials, blind signatures, and verifiable encryption for offline peer-to-peer payments. Implemented using C++, the pqzk-blns framework, and Flask for entity web interfaces.

  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs & Quantum Networking

    Extensive exploration of zk-SNARKs (Groth16), BBS signatures, and KZG commitments. Additionally simulated entanglement-based quantum networking schemes (BB84, E91) and quantum blind signature protocols using Python.
